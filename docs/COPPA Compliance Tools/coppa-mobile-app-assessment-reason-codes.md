#### COPPA Violation Risk Assessment Reason Codes (Mobile)

The reason code descriptions below correspond to each numbered row in grid above.

  1. The COPPA risk is**low** because the app is likely not directed to children under 13.
  2. The COPPA risk is**medium** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, and it has sensitive permissions. The app has a privacy policy detected as required by the COPPA Rule. 
  3. The COPPA risk is **medium** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, but does not have sensitive permissions. The app has a privacy policy detected.
  4. The COPPA risk is **high** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, it has sensitive permissions, and it passes residential IP data. The app has a privacy policy detected.
  5. The COPPA risk is **high** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, it has sensitive permissions, and it passes location information. The app has a privacy policy detected.
  6. The COPPA risk is **high** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, and it passes location information. The app has a privacy policy detected.
  7. The COPPA risk is **high** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, and it passes residential IP data. The app has a privacy policy detected.
  8. The COPPA risk is **critical** because the app is likely directed to children under 13, and it does not have a privacy policy detected as required by the COPPA rule.
