### Websites

**Tooltip/Label** | **Description**  
---|---  
SECTION: IVT OVERVIEW |   
IVT Overview | The overall percentage of Invalid Traffic (IVT) associated with advertising transactions for the domain. All IVT calculations are derived from standardized definitions of invalid traffic maintained by the Media Rating Council, Inc. (MRC), Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB), and Trustworthy Accountability Group (TAG).  
SECTION: RISK OVERVIEW |   
Risk Overview | Pixalate's unique advertising risk assessment based on blended risk factors including brand safety, invalid traffic and ad inventory  
Desktop | Blended advertiser risk assessment for the Domain on Desktops by global business region  
Mobile | Blended advertiser risk assessment for the Domain on Mobile Phones by global business region  
Pixalate Advisories | Pixalate's unique listing of key advisories for a particular domain based on risk factors (e.g., ads.txt, privacy terms, etc.).  
SECTION: MADE FOR ADVERTISING (MFA) RISK |   
MFA Risk | Pixalate's assessment of the likelihood of the website being deemed MFA, based on Pixalate's MFA detection methodology.  
Ad Refresh Rate | Number of ad impressions presented to a user, per minute.  
Ad Density | Number of ad placements presented to a user, per minute.  
Social Traffic Rate | Based on the amount of traffic sourced via social media.  
Paid Traffic Rate | Based on the amount of traffic sourced via paid (ad) sources  
SECTION: SITE RISK |   
WHOIS Info Hidden | Private Domains are those that are registered as anonymous in the Whois site registration data  
Corporate Email | Indicates if the app has a significant amount of impressions detected that may be misrepresented in advertising transactions.  
SECTION: BRAND SAFETY RISK |   
Brand Safety Risk | Risk levels for our brand safety categories are assessed at the homepage (root domain) level  
SECTION: SOCIAL MEDIA ENGAGEMENT |   
Social Media Engagement | Active engagement levels for various social platforms  
SECTION: INVALID TRAFFIC (IVT) |   
GIVT% | General Invalid Traffic - Deterministically judged to be non-human traffic; high to very high risk  
SIVT% | Sophisticated Invalid Traffic - Probabilistically judged to be non-human traffic; moderate to high risk traffic  
SECTION: VIEWABILITY |   
Global Viewability | The average viewability percentage of valid traffic for all ad sizes and traffic types in the app  
SECTION: REFERRAL TRAFFIC |   
Social | The percent of this domain's traffic that comes from social media  
Direct | The percent of this domain's traffic that comes from a user directly typing the web address in their browser or clicking an existing bookmark  
Search | The percent of this domain's traffic that comes from search engines  
Discovery | The percent of this domain's traffic that comes from widgets or links to this site from other websites  
Email | The percent of this domain's traffic that comes from email

### MRT Discovery

**Tooltip/Label** | **Description**  
---|---  
SELECTION: Connected TV (Roku, Fire TV, Samsung, tvOS) |   
MFA Risk | Pixalate's assessment of the risk of the app being deemed MFA, based on Pixalate's MFA detection methodology  
SELECTION: Mobile (Google Play. Apple App Store) |   
Est. Inventory | The estimated number of monthly impressions this app makes available programmatically.  
Viewability | The average viewability for all display ad impressions in the app  
Est. IVT | Percentage of Invalid Traffic associated with advertising transactions for the app  
Blocklisted | The app has been placed on a Pixalate high risk blocklist - contact sales for subscription details  
MFA Risk | Pixalate's assessment of the risk of the app being deemed MFA, based on Pixalate's MFA detection methodology  
Brand Safety Risk | Risk factors to advertiser Brand Safety broken down by both in-app content such as images, and descriptive text about the app  
Incentivized App | Indicates if the app is an incentivized app  
User Generated Content | Indicates if the app has user generated content  
SELECTION: Domains |   
Viewability | The average viewability percentage of valid traffic for all ad sizes and traffic types in the domain  
IVT | The total percentage of non-human traffic measured on the domain
