### **Seller Trust Index Overview**

This section provides a snapshot of the seller’s trust, performance, compliance, and market position, highlighting key metrics to quickly evaluate seller quality and reliability.

  * **Final Score** : A combined trust rating (0–100), presented with letter grades (A-F) for easy interpretation.
  * **SIVT Score** : Measures the risk of encountering Sophisticated Invalid Traffic.
  * **GIVT Score** : Reflects the risk of General Invalid Traffic.
  * **SCO Verification Score** : Indicates compliance with Supply Chain Object standards.
  * **Market Share Score** : Shows the seller’s market position compared to competitors.
  * **MFA Score** : Identifies risk related to Made-for-Advertising inventory.
  * **SPO Score** : Evaluates the efficiency and optimization of the seller’s supply paths.

### **Seller JSON Details**

This section provides technical information derived from the seller’s JSON file, which includes metadata about the seller’s authorized partnerships and operational details:

  * **Version:** Indicates the current JSON file version.
  * **Contact Information:** Seller’s official email and physical address.
  * **Crawled by Pixalate:** The date when Pixalate last verified the seller’s JSON file.
  * **Associated URLs:** Websites officially linked to the seller.
  * **Relationship Breakdown:** Clearly shows the counts of Publishers, Intermediaries, and entities listed as both, helping you quickly gauge the seller’s network composition.

### Commonly Asked Questions

  * How often is the seller insights page updated? 
    * The seller insights page is updated on a weekly basis but note that some portions may have different update cadences due to the data source it is pulling from (eg. Seller Trust Index criteria will be generated on a quarterly basis).
  * What is the basis for top authorized apps? 
    * Top authorized apps are sorted on the basis of impression volume while the number of apps listed is based on the crawled app-ads.txt or ads.txt files. 
  * Is the seller insights page open to the public? 
    * At the moment, viewing seller insight details require an MRT subscription.
